Colorado conditions

Built for freeze-thaw and clay soil.

Their driveway page names the local problems: cracking from freeze-thaw, settling on expansive clay, scaling from de-icing salts, and poor drainage toward the house. The published fixes are reinforcement and control joints, a compacted gravel base, a low water-to-cement mix with proper cure, and grading that sends water away.
